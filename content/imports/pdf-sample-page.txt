Passage One

Remote work has changed how teams communicate. Some employees enjoy the flexibility, while others miss the office.

46. What is mentioned about remote work?
A) It changed how teams communicate.
B) It reduced office costs.
C) It is popular with everyone.
D) It has no disadvantages.
答案：A
解析：文章第一句指出遠距工作改變了團隊溝通方式。
